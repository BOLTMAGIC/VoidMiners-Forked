# 🌌 VoidMiners Reforged

**Harness the infinite power of the void with advanced multiblock mining and solar energy systems!**

---

## 🎯 Overview

VoidMiners Reforged is a comprehensive mod that adds sophisticated void mining and solar energy generation systems to Minecraft. Create powerful multiblock structures to extract resources from the void itself, while supporting your operations with advanced solar panels that harness celestial energy.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🔮 **9-Tier Crystal Progression System**
Experience a complete progression journey through 9 distinct crystal tiers, each with unique properties and increasing power:

- **🔴 Rubetine** - Entry-level void mining
- **🟠 Aurantium** - Enhanced efficiency
- **🟡 Citrinetine** - Improved speed and capacity
- **🟢 Verdium** - Balanced performance boost
- **🔵 Azurine** - High-efficiency operations
- **🟣 Caerium** - Advanced technology
- **💜 Amethystine** - Superior capabilities
- **🌸 Rosarium** - Elite-tier performance
- **⭐ Ultimate** - Maximum power and efficiency

### ⛏️ **Void Miners**
- **Multiblock Structures**: Build complex mining installations using frames, panels, and control blocks
- **Automatic Resource Generation**: Extract valuable materials directly from the void
- **Energy-Powered**: Requires substantial power to operate (300 FE/t to 700 FE/t)
- **Massive Storage**: Energy capacities from 10 MFE to over 2.1 GFE
- **Configurable Speed**: Mining intervals from 1000 ticks (Rubetine) to 200 ticks (Ultimate)

---

### ☀️ **Solar Panel Systems**
- **Parallel Progression**: Each crystal tier has corresponding solar technology
- **Efficient Power Generation**: Generate 20 FE/t to 5,120 FE/t clean energy
- **Weather Adaptive**: Built-in weather resistance systems
- **Massive Capacities**: Store from 5 MFE to 2.1 GFE of solar energy
- **Modular Design**: Expandable multiblock solar installations

### 🔧 **Advanced Modifier System**

#### For Miners:
- **⚡ Energy Modifiers**: Reduce power consumption by up to 35% (Ultimate tier)
- **🚀 Speed Modifiers**: Increase mining speed by up to 30% (Ultimate tier)
- **📦 Item Modifiers**: Multiply resource output by up to 2.5x (Ultimate tier)

#### For Solar Panels:
- **⚡ Efficiency Modifiers**: Boost power generation by up to 35%
- **🌦️ Weather Resistance**: Reduce weather impact by up to 233%


## 🏗️ Multiblock Construction

Each tier requires specific multiblock patterns:
- **Frame Blocks**: Structural foundation for your installations
- **Panel Blocks**: Functional components for operations
- **Glass Panels**: Transparent elements for complex designs
- **Controller Blocks**: Central management units
- **Modifier Blocks**: Enhancement modules

*Tip: Use JEI integration to view exact multiblock patterns and requirements!*

## ⚙️ Configuration

VoidMiners Reforged is fully configurable through `void-miners.json5`:
- Adjust energy requirements and capacities
- Modify mining speeds and generation rates
- Customize modifier effects and bonuses
- Enable/disable energy requirements for testing
- Fine-tune progression balance
